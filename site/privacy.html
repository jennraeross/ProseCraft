Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

ProseCraft is a writing application available as a desktop app and in a web browser. This policy describes information handled by the app and the ProseCraft website.

Information you create

Your projects, notes, settings, and other writing are stored on your device. In the browser app, project data is kept in that browser's local storage and IndexedDB; it is not sent to a ProseCraft-operated server. Clearing browser data or removing the app may delete local projects, so keep your own backups.

Optional sync and Google Drive

If you choose Google Drive sync, ProseCraft uses Google's sign-in and Drive services to access the app's private Drive app-data area. Google may receive information such as your Google account details and authorization data as part of that process. Google's handling of that information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

In encrypted sync modes, project data is encrypted in ProseCraft before it is uploaded; keep your passphrase safe because it is needed to access that data on another device. Open-folder sync is not encrypted by ProseCraft and may store readable project data in Drive. Your Google Drive account and its settings control that data. You can disconnect Drive in the app and revoke ProseCraft's access through your Google Account permissions.

The browser app stores sync credentials and preferences in browser storage on your device. Anyone with access to your browser profile or device may be able to access that stored information.

Website hosting and diagnostics

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. When your browser requests the site, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address and request details to provide, secure, and operate its hosting. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details. ProseCraft does not use advertising trackers or analytics on this website.

ProseCraft does not operate a service that collects your writing or create an account for you. If you choose to contact the project through GitHub, the information you post is handled by GitHub and may be public; do not include sensitive information in public issues.

Your choices and data deletion

You can delete local project data using the app's controls or your browser's site-data settings. You can remove synced files from your Google Drive account; disconnecting Drive alone does not delete files already stored there. The desktop app may store data in its own local application storage, separate from the browser app.

Children

ProseCraft is not directed to children, and the project does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a ProseCraft-operated account or server.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as ProseCraft changes. Updates will be posted on this page. For questions, contact the project through GitHub Issues. Please do not post private or sensitive information there.
